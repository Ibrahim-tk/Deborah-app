Intake Spec v1.2: Design and Dev Responsibilities

Proposal from the Design team to the Delivery Lead
Your Oracle Clinician, 27 October 2026

Summary

Design owns how the intake looks and feels: wording, placement, screens and demo paths. The AI/Dev team owns the logic: which question is shown when, warning rules, and what the AI is told. Design work is 2 days.

1. Why we're proposing this

#Reason
1The prototype is a design tool. It has no real backend and no real AI.
2Most of the v1.2 spec is conditional logic: which question appears for which body, age, stage or gender. The real engine has to do this anyway.
3Building the same logic in the prototype would create two versions of the rules, which would drift apart.
4Design decides how a question appears. The engine decides whether it appears.

3. AI / Dev team responsibilities

AreaWhat it means
Question branching rulesWhich question shows for which organs, sex at birth, stage, age and gender. Never route by gender alone. "Prefer not to say" never blocks.
Chat type flag (A/B/C/D)Self, child, older parent, partner. Set at the start of each chat and passed to the AI as fixed context. Never guessed or changed mid-chat.
Warning levels by chat typeThe same symptoms give different urgency depending on who the chat is about, including the older-parent UTI / sepsis rule.
Answer logicN/A pre-set on the symptom list, required vs. optional fields, no repeated questions between the profile and the chat.
What the AI is toldWhich profile answers go to the AI, pronouns and body terms in replies, and the content of the provider summary.
Data and privacyEncryption, no sensitive fields sent to analytics or Shopify, edit and delete for each answer, data handling for under-13s and proxies.
PlatformStripe payments, social login, and syncing new articles into the knowledge base.
